# Glossary

**Carrier.** The part of the SettleIn download that holds the selection on a Mac where SettleIn is not yet installed.

**Catalog.** The list of apps SettleIn offers. The owner approves every entry.

**Catalog change.** A release notes entry for apps added, removed or changed on settlein.dev. It needs no app update.

**Confirmation screen.** Removed by ADR-0034. It was the screen in the app that showed the selection and waited for approval before anything downloaded.

**Design handoff.** The designer's final package in `docs/design/`: decisions, design system, states, copy, accessibility notes, tokens, assets, wireframes and diagrams. The screenshots stay in the owner's Downloads.

**Design handoff package.** The brief and grey-box wireframes that the outside designer read before designing. The design handoff replaced it.

**Handoff.** The step where the website tells the SettleIn app which apps the person ticked.

**Leftovers.** The support files, caches and preferences an app leaves on the Mac after its bundle is gone.

**Notice.** A release notes entry about a catalog app, such as an app paused after it failed a check.

**Pick apps.** The picker inside the Mac app, screen A7. It has the website picker's layout and marks installed apps.

**Pkg app.** A catalog app that ships as a `.pkg` installer. The person clicks through Apple's Installer to install it.

**Scroll story.** The website home page, where scrolling zooms the hero's demo window into the full-screen picker.

**Selection.** The set of apps a person ticked on the website or in Pick apps.

**SettleIn.** The product name, with a capital I. The domain stays settlein.dev.
